Kubernetes can't limit access to TLS Secrets alone: an account that may read them may read every Secret, including passwords. k0s-monitor watches only TLS Secrets, keeps only their certificate (names, issuer and dates) and drops the private key as it arrives. It reads another Secret only when someone opens it on the Secrets pages, and shows values only if that is turned on in Settings: one at a time, each recorded in the audit log.
